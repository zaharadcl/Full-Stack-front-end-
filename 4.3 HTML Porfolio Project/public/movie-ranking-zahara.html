The Best Series According to Zahara

my top 3 series of all time

Game Of Thrones

no words for this series, I love with all my heart the whole universe, House of the Dragons and The Knight of the Seven Kingdoms, excited for the rest.

Modern Family

I love sitcoms and without a doubt this is my favorite, lots of laughs and many life lessons.

Lord Of The Rings

A classic that could not be missing. I've watched the extended trilogy more than eight times, and it never gets old. I used to watch it with my father when I was a child, so it brings back many great memories. It was this trilogy that sparked my passion for fantasy, science fiction, anime, and the geek universe as a whole.
